How to request account deletion

ToolCare users can request deletion of their account and associated ToolCare data by contacting Thomassen Solutions.

Send an email to Dthomassen@pm.me with the subject ToolCare account deletion request.

Please include:

  • The email address used for your ToolCare account.
  • Your company name, if applicable.
  • A short confirmation that you want your ToolCare account deleted.

We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account before deleting data.

What data may be deleted

Depending on your role and company setup, deletion may include your account profile, login access, notification preferences, and personal information connected to your ToolCare account.

Business records and retention

ToolCare is a business maintenance app. Some customer, tool, service, maintenance, attachment, audit, or company records may need to be retained by the company owner or by Thomassen Solutions where required for security, legal, operational, or legitimate business reasons.

Processing time

We aim to respond to account deletion requests within 30 days.
