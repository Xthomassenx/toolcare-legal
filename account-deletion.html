ToolCare Account Deletion

Last updated: June 07, 2026

This page explains how users can request deletion of their ToolCare account and associated data. ToolCare is operated by Thomassen Solutions.

To request deletion of your ToolCare account, email us at Dthomassen@pm.me with the subject ToolCare account deletion request.

What to include in your request

Please include enough information for us to identify your account:

  • Your ToolCare login email address
  • Your company name, if applicable
  • A short confirmation that you want your ToolCare account deleted

What data will be deleted

When your deletion request is verified, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your ToolCare account, including:

  • Account/profile information such as name and email address
  • Notification preferences linked to your user account
  • Access permissions linked to your user account

Company, customer, tool, maintenance, and attachment records may belong to the company workspace. If you are requesting deletion of a full company workspace, please state this clearly in the request.

Data retention

Some limited information may be retained for a reasonable period where required for legal, security, backup, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes.

Processing time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days unless applicable law requires or allows a different period.

Contact

Email: Dthomassen@pm.me

You may also submit a privacy/data request using Termly's request form: Data subject access request.
